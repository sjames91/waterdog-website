About Waterdog

Our mission

Everyone deserves access to high-quality learning resources.

Teachers spend countless hours planning, building materials, tracking data, and grading before they ever get to teach. Waterdog takes that heavy lifting off their plate, so teachers can focus on what they do best: teaching.

What we're building

  • Curriculum - curriculum maps, units, and ready-to-use lessons
  • Data tools - simple ways to track student progress
  • Accessible testing - test-taking tools that work for every learner
  • Classroom tools - grading and learning management

We're still building. New resources are added as they're ready.
